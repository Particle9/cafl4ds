# P1.2 — Does online adaptation beat an init-matched frozen backbone?

## The value proposition

The coupled project assumes that live self-supervised updates improve the representation. P1.2 tests that assumption
directly. Every live model is compared with an exact frozen twin that starts from the same weights and sees the same
held-out evaluation examples. A null result is existential: the published plan says to stop the coupled adaptation path
and study selection efficiency instead.

## Results

Neither tested TinyViT SSL vehicle passes. The joint-embedding study spans random, in-domain warm, and independently
pretrained starts, ascending-label probes, replay, semantic deduplication, cached-feature distillation, last-block and
residual-adapter scopes, preserved pretrained heads, and a fixed-teacher plasticity-plus-retention objective. Positive
cells are seed- or label-budget-specific; none earns confirmation.

The primary forgetting vehicle, warm TinyViT–MAE, was then tested over the fixed development seeds with three learning
rates and two update cadences. Every one of the six cells records 0/3 strict joint wins. Low-pressure cells preserve the
representation but do not improve it. Higher pressure causes measurable drift and sometimes a kNN gain, but linear
accuracy falls and earlier-condition retention does not improve consistently. No cell advances to five-seed
confirmation.

This is a bounded result. It closes the currently scoped small-model implementations; it does not claim that a larger,
strongly pretrained MAE or a materially different continual objective must fail.

## The one-line takeaway

For both tested TinyViT SSL vehicles, live adaptation is either inert or less useful than freezing, so the scoped
project should reframe to selection/communication efficiency unless it explicitly funds a higher-capacity
continual-learning vehicle.

## Full BDD100K train/validation pilot — 2026-09-21

The small validation-stream studies left open a simple data-dose objection. A split-safe full-data path was therefore
added: official BDD100K `train` frames are the only online-update source, official `val` frames are the only probe
source, and `test` remains untouched. The current mirror's native `labels/<split>/*.json` layout is read directly. After
undefined-attribute removal and the predeclared 50-frame scene floor, the run used 61,555 train images across 18 driving
regimes and 8,753 validation images across the same 18 regimes. Four train scenes and three validation scenes remained
probeable.

The first bounded pilot used seed 11, from-scratch TinyViT–MAE, full-backbone updates every batch, `lr=1e-3`, and an
exact frozen twin. Configuration: `cafl4ds/configs/adaptation_bdd_full_mae.yaml`; artifacts:
`outputs/adaptation-bdd/full-mae/20260921-full-pilot-s11/`.

| Read | Adapted | Frozen | Gain |
| -- | -: | -: | -: |
| Global kNN | 41.33% | 45.33% | -4.00 pp |
| Global linear | 41.33% | 49.33% | -8.00 pp |
| Mean final condition | — | — | -1.90 pp |

The SSL objective clearly optimized (`1.2820 -> 0.5871` reconstruction loss) and RankMe rose (`4.51 -> 12.16`), but
useful held-out representation quality did not follow. Backward transfer was `-0.0582` and the forgetting measure was
`0.1369`. Few-shot gains were mixed: -1.33 pp (1-shot), -5.33 pp (2-shot), +10.67 pp (5-shot), +4.00 pp (10-shot),
-12.00 pp (20-shot), and -8.00 pp (25-shot).

**Pilot verdict: No Go for this from-scratch full-data cell.** This rejects the narrow claim that simply exposing the
existing TinyViT–MAE online learner to the full BDD100K train split is enough. Because this is one seed and starts from
random weights, it is not the confirmation test for a competent pretrained backbone and does not establish a general
BDD100K or continual-learning failure. Do not spend the remaining seed budget on this exact cell; the next informative
comparison must change initialization/capacity or constrain plasticity, while retaining the independent train/validation
protocol.

## Clean train-only warm-start pilot — 2026-09-21

The random-initialization confound was then removed without evaluation leakage. Official BDD train was deterministically
split within every `(regime, scene)` cell: 20% for stationary MAE warm-up and the complementary 80% for the one-pass
nonstationary stream. Official validation remained the only probe source and test remained untouched. The train-only
well used 12,315 images, plateaued after 118 updates (`1.0484 -> 0.8678`), and was saved as the complete MAE method at
`outputs/p1.2/wells/mae_bdd_train20_s101.pt`.

Seed 101 compared two online scopes with an exact frozen twin of that well. Full-backbone adaptation used `lr=1e-4`;
last-block-only adaptation used `lr=3e-4`. Artifacts: `outputs/adaptation-bdd/clean-warm-mae/20260921-pilot-s101/`.

| Scope | kNN gain | Linear gain | Mean current gain | Final past gain | BWT | Forgetting |
| -- | -: | -: | -: | -: | -: | -: |
| Full backbone | +10.67 pp | +9.33 pp | +5.77 pp | +0.60 pp | -4.67 pp | 12.61 pp |
| Last block | +4.00 pp | -6.67 pp | +1.52 pp | +2.33 pp | +0.72 pp | 5.16 pp |

Full adaptation also improved five of six few-shot reads: -1.33 pp (1-shot), +10.67 pp (2-shot), +1.33 pp (5-shot),
+10.67 pp (10-shot), +6.67 pp (20-shot), and +9.33 pp (25-shot). This is the first full-BDD cell to show substantial
useful adaptation over an initialization-matched competent frozen model. It also exhibits meaningful within-stream
forgetting: final performance on earlier conditions remains slightly above frozen on average, but declines from their
earlier adapted peaks.

Last-block training supplies the complementary mechanism result. Constraining plasticity sharply reduces forgetting and
yields positive BWT, but loses global linear quality and several few-shot budgets. The result is therefore a genuine
stability-plasticity tradeoff, not evidence that the low-pressure arm solves P1.2.

**Pilot verdict: promising but not promoted under the complete gate.** The full arm clears global kNN, global linear,
current-condition, and most few-shot criteria, but misses the no-material- forgetting criterion. The last-block arm
clears retention but misses linear quality. Since this is one seed, it does not overturn the earlier multi-seed No-Go.
It does justify a targeted retention intervention on the useful full-backbone arm (for example compute-matched replay),
rather than more random-init seeds or another unconstrained learning-rate sweep.

## Compute-matched replay retention pilot — 2026-09-22

The successful full-backbone clean-warm arm was rerun with the same seed, warm well, 80% train stream, validation
probes, learning rate, and full-backbone scope, changing only the selector to a compute-matched reservoir replay arm.
Every incoming frame entered a 256-frame reservoir; once warm, the optimizer saw 16 current plus 16 replayed frames,
capped at the same 32-image training batch as the no-replay control. Configuration:
`cafl4ds/configs/adaptation_bdd_clean_warm_mae_replay.yaml`; artifacts:
`outputs/adaptation-bdd/clean-warm-mae-replay/20260921-s101/`.

| Read | No replay | Replay | Replay effect |
| -- | -: | -: | -: |
| Global kNN gain | +10.67 pp | +8.00 pp | lower, still positive |
| Global linear gain | +9.33 pp | 0.00 pp | lower |
| Mean final condition gain | +1.36 pp | **+5.50 pp** | improved |
| Backward transfer | -4.67 pp | **+1.73 pp** | improved |
| Forgetting | 12.61 pp | **4.22 pp** | reduced |

Replay's few-shot gains were +4.00 pp (1-shot), +19.00 pp (2-shot), +7.00 pp (5-shot), -4.00 pp (10-shot), +7.00 pp
(20-shot), and 0.00 pp (25-shot). It therefore preserves the useful current/ past-condition behavior more reliably, but
gives up the no-replay arm's large global linear gain.

**Interpretation: replay is a successful retention intervention, not a universal improvement.** It turns the
full-backbone arm from negative BWT/12.61 pp forgetting into positive BWT/4.22 pp forgetting at fixed compute, while
retaining positive kNN and condition gains. This is the strongest current candidate for three-seed confirmation. The
next run should confirm the no-replay and replay arms on fresh seeds, with the primary decision focused on the
retention-adjusted joint criterion.

## Three-seed clean-warm replay confirmation — 2026-09-22

The proposed confirmation was completed on fresh seeds `103`, `107`, and `109` for both arms. The train-only warm well,

**Audit correction, 2026-09-27: the learning rates did not match.** The saved manifests record `lr=1e-3` for no replay

`outputs/adaptation-bdd/clean-warm-confirmation/20260922/full/` and
`outputs/adaptation-bdd/clean-warm-confirmation/20260922/replay/`.

| Arm (3 seeds) | Global kNN gain | Global linear gain | Mean final-condition gain | Mean BWT | Mean forgetting |
| -- | -: | -: | -: | -: | -: |
| Full backbone, no replay | **+4.00 pp** (3/3 wins) | -6.22 pp (1/3 wins) | +1.68 pp | +3.46 pp | 11.40 pp |
| Full backbone, replay | **+11.11 pp** (3/3 wins) | -1.33 pp (1/3 wins) | **+3.46 pp** | -0.03 pp | **6.74 pp** |

The replay recipe has 4.66 pp less mean forgetting and larger mean kNN/final-condition gains than the no-replay recipe.
Because learning rate also changed, this ensemble does **not** isolate replay's causal effect or confirm the pilot's
matched-recipe retention claim. Likewise the difference from the no-replay pilot cannot be attributed to seeds alone.
The earlier one-seed pilot did match learning rates; its retention finding remains exploratory.

Each arm's comparison against its exact frozen twin remains interpretable. The replay recipe wins kNN on all three
seeds, but its mean global linear gain is negative and only one seed wins that readout. It is a candidate advantage on
specific measures, not a complete P1.2 result. "Mean final-condition gain" averages conditions at the final checkpoint;
it is not accuracy on the last regime alone.

The [2026-09-27 fixed confirmation protocol](../phase1/P1.2-replay-confirmation.md) tests the unchanged replay recipe
against frozen on five fresh training seeds and larger repeated evaluation panels. It does not compare replay against no
replay, so it cannot repair the earlier causal-comparison gap by itself.

## Matched five-seed replay ablation — 2026-09-27

Completed the [replay causal ablation](../phase1/P1.2-replay-causal-ablation.md) on seeds
`2003, 2011, 2017, 2027, 2029`. The existing replay/frozen runs were reused and five matching AcceptAll (no-replay) runs
were added. Both online arms used the same TinyViT-MAE warm checkpoint, `lr=1e-4`, full-backbone updates, 49,240-image
train stream, and 32-image batch limit. Replay used a 256-image reservoir and up to 16 historical images per step,
displacing current images within that limit. This estimates the effect of this complete replay policy at a fixed batch
limit.

Evaluation used 75 support images and 1,500 global queries per panel from official validation, with three panels
averaged within each training seed. The intervals below are paired 95% Student-t intervals over five seeds, conditional
on this warm checkpoint, dataset, regime order, and panel set. Saved configuration checks passed, and all frozen
panel-level aggregate scores matched exactly.

| Comparison | kNN gain, pp [95% interval] | Linear gain, pp [95% interval] | Mean final-condition gain, pp [95% interval] |
| -- | -: | -: | -: |
| Replay minus no replay | -0.07 [-0.62, +0.48] | +0.07 [-0.76, +0.89] | -0.45 [-2.20, +1.30] |
| Replay minus frozen | +1.03 [+0.68, +1.37] | -0.22 [-0.68, +0.24] | -0.43 [-2.04, +1.18] |
| No replay minus frozen | +1.10 [+0.69, +1.50] | -0.28 [-0.91, +0.34] | +0.02 [-0.94, +0.97] |

Replay forgetting was 7.79 pp versus 7.57 pp without replay. The paired difference was +0.22 pp [-0.53, +0.97], where
positive means more forgetting. Replay BWT was -3.92 pp versus -3.72 pp without replay; its paired difference was -0.20
pp [-3.27, +2.87]. These retention measurements use panel 0. Rare conditions have as few as 1–5 query images and receive
equal weight in the condition macro-average, so retention and condition results warrant particular caution.

**Verdict: replay's added benefit is not established.** All three direct replay-versus-no-replay accuracy intervals
cross zero; no retention benefit was established either. Both online arms show a small positive mean kNN gain over
frozen under these panels, but neither establishes broad accuracy superiority. This closes the learning-rate confound
for this recipe; it does not prove equivalence or show that replay generally fails. In particular, it does not identify
model size, replay capacity, probe support size, or optimization as the cause.

The panel dependence is material: the replay arm's mean kNN gain was +3.00 pp on panel 0, but +0.20 and -0.12 pp on the
two other panels. Its linear gains were +1.81, -2.95, and +0.48 pp. Hence the narrow seed intervals should not be
interpreted as uncertainty over new datasets or arbitrary probe panels. The earlier small-panel gains are not robust
evidence of broad superiority.

Artifacts: `outputs/adaptation-bdd/replay-causal-ablation/20260927/causal_summary.json`, `causal_summary.md`, and
`causal.png`; individual model checkpoints and comparison reports remain in each seed directory. Run
`python -m scripts.confirm_replay_causal` to execute/resume the matched arm, or add `--summarize-only` to recalculate
from completed outputs. Plot with `python -m scripts.plot_replay_causal <path-to-causal_summary.json>`.

The next development study should compare a less-plastic update rule against both frozen and the full-backbone no-replay
control, with multiple probe panels retained. This is a new hypothesis requiring development and fresh confirmation; it
is not established by the present results.

## Matched last-block adaptation — 2026-09-27 (reviewed 2026-09-28)

Completed all five seeds in the [restricted-plasticity protocol](../phase1/P1.2-last-block-comparison.md). The

position/class tokens, and first three blocks remain frozen. It uses the same train-only warm TinyViT-MAE checkpoint,
49,240-image stream, AdamW learning rate of 1e-4, batch limit of 32, 1,547 updates, and no replay as the full-backbone

computation is lower.

Three validation panels were averaged within each of the five reused training seeds. These paired 95% Student-t
intervals describe seed variation conditional on the shared checkpoint and panels, not uncertainty over arbitrary

+4.29\], did not establish improved BWT. Both retention measurements use panel 0, with sparse condition queries and
equal condition weights.

**The predeclared retention/accuracy development screen passed.** Forgetting decreased and all three accuracy-difference
intervals against full-backbone learning had lower limits above -1 pp. This allows a small accuracy loss: linear-probe

Panel dependence remains substantial. Last-block minus frozen kNN gains were -0.88, +1.55, and +1.52 pp across panels 0,

Saved configurations matched after accounting for the intended scope and run metadata, and frozen panel scores matched

## Checkpoint evaluation robustness — completed 2026-09-30

the common warm checkpoint. It fixes disjoint query/support pools across every method, varies labels per class (5, 25,
100), and averages three nested support draws within training seed. Readouts are kNN, linear probing and genuine
supervised fine-tuning of independent copies of the entire backbone. The original checkpoints and stream histories are
not modified. This addresses P1.2 downstream robustness and the project plan's distinction between frozen probes and
few-shot fine-tuning, not a new independent confirmation or a new forgetting trajectory.

user approved temporary sleep prevention for this run. The runner saves per-cell predictions, support-only training
audits, immutable input/pool fingerprints, progress, and eventual aggregate JSON/Markdown/figure outputs. The fixed 189
